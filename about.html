Our story

Built on-site, one installation at a time

ADC Systems Integrated Solutions Limited grew out of everyday jobs, wiring a shop's cameras, fixing a home's power backup, fitting a smart lock on a gate. Every project taught us something about what Lagos properties actually need, and that experience now shapes how we scope, quote and install every job.

We keep installation in-house rather than subcontracting, so the technician who surveys your property is the same team that wires and commissions it.

Hands-on installation

Our own engineers wire and configure every job, start to finish.

Genuine equipment

We install branded, warrantied hardware, not unmarked substitutes.

Timely delivery

Site visits are scheduled and jobs completed on the date agreed.

Support after install

We stay reachable for troubleshooting once the job is done.
