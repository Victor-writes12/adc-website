Common questions

Before you book a site visit

Do you supply the equipment, or do I buy it myself?

We supply and install genuine equipment as part of every quote, sourced directly so you get warranty support through us.

How long does a typical CCTV or solar installation take?

Most home CCTV or inverter installs are completed in a single day. Larger commercial jobs are scoped and scheduled during the site visit.

Do you offer maintenance after installation?

Yes, we offer maintenance plans and remain on call for troubleshooting, settings changes, and equipment servicing.

Can you combine CCTV, solar, and smart home into one job?

Yes, this is common. One site visit can scope all three, and our engineers coordinate the wiring so systems don't conflict.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us about your property and we'll recommend a setup during a free visit.
